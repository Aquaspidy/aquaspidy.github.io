What It Does

Voop's Athletic Performance module tracks every player's movement in real time using computer vision and persistent tracking. The system monitors speed, distance covered, sprint frequency, and running time for each player on the court, providing coaches with objective data on player workload and effort.

Beyond basic tracking, Voop calculates a fatigue index for each player based on sprint count, total distance, and recent speed decline. This helps coaches identify when players are tiring before it becomes visibly apparent — enabling smarter substitution decisions.

The system also tracks team-level metrics including total distance, average speed, and possession time, giving coaches a complete picture of their team's physical output across the game.

How It Works

📍 Position Tracking

YOLOv8 detects player positions every frame. Persistent tracking maintains player identities across the game.

📐 Distance Calculation

Calculates distance moved between frames using Euclidean distance, accumulating total distance per player and team.

💨 Sprint Detection

Speed >5.5 km/h triggers sprint tracking. Sprint duration and count are logged for intensity analysis.

🪫 Fatigue Index

Combines sprint count, total distance, and speed decline to calculate a 0-100 fatigue score. Flags players at risk of exhaustion.

In-Game Application

Coaches often rely on "eye test" to judge player fatigue — but players can hide their tiredness or coaches might miss subtle signs. Voop's fatigue index provides objective data on when a player's performance is declining.

When a player's fatigue exceeds 70%, Voop flags them for substitution. The system also tracks sprint counts — if a key player is sprinting 30+ times in a half, they may need rest. Post-game reports show which players covered the most distance, who had the highest sprint count, and which lineups were most efficient in terms of movement-to-production ratio.

This data also helps in practice planning — if players consistently fatigue at the same point in games, coaches can design conditioning drills to extend their effective minutes.
