What It Does

Voop's Live Matchup feature tracks which player has the ball and identifies the closest defender in real time. Using YOLOv8 object detection and persistent player tracking, the system maintains player identities across frames, allowing it to follow specific players throughout the game.

When a player receives the ball, Voop instantly identifies the nearest defender and displays the matchup side-by-side. The system then analyzes the matchup for advantages — comparing player height, speed, and positioning to detect potential mismatches that coaches might miss while watching the ball.

Mismatches are flagged with visual alerts, giving coaches immediate awareness of favorable matchups anywhere on the court. Over the course of the game, Voop tracks which players are being targeted, helping teams exploit defensive weaknesses and adjust assignments accordingly.

How It Works

Ball Detection
Identifies which player has possession based on ball proximity and movement patterns.
Defender Proximity
Calculates distance from ball handler to every opponent, identifying the closest defender.
Mismatch Analysis
Compares height ratio, speed differential, and positioning to identify advantages.
Post-Game Reports
Logs every matchup and mismatch occurrence for detailed post-game analysis.

In-Game Application

Coaches can't watch every matchup simultaneously. By using Voop, mismatches are labeled clearly in real time across the entire court. When a mismatch is detected — such as a taller offensive player guarded by a shorter defender — a visual alert appears, allowing coaches to immediately direct the ball toward the advantage without pausing to review film.

The system also tracks defensive assignments, helping coaches identify which defenders are being targeted most effectively and whether their team is hunting mismatches or settling for even matchups. Post-game reports show which players consistently created advantages and which opponents were exploited most effectively.
