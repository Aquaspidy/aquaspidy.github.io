ADVANTAGE DETECTION

Mismatch Hunter

Automatically highlights favorable defensive mismatches — because coaches can't watch every matchup at once.

Voop automatically identifies favorable defensive mismatches by comparing adjacent players' heights and positions. When a taller offensive player gets guarded by a shorter defender, or a quicker guard faces a slower big, the system draws colored lines highlighting the advantage.

How Mismatches Are Detected

📐
Vertical Separation

Compares bounding box heights to calculate height advantage

📍
Position Tracking

Analyzes which defenders are assigned to which offensive players

⚡
Sub-Second Triggers

Mismatch detection activates within seconds of formation

How It Works

📊 Height Comparison

Compares bounding box heights of adjacent players. A height ratio above 1.25 triggers mismatch detection.

🎯 Position Analysis

Analyzes which defender is guarding which offensive player using spatial positioning and tracking IDs.

🟡 Colored Lines

Draws orange/red highlight lines connecting mismatched players for immediate visual recognition.

📝 Mismatch Logging

Every forced mismatch is logged with timestamps, players involved, and whether it led to a scoring opportunity.

What Voop Highlights

Post Mismatch

Big guarded by smaller defender — immediate post-up advantage anywhere on the court.

Speed Mismatch

Quick guard isolated against slower big — drive and finish advantage.

Switch Aftermath

Detects mismatches created by defensive switches, even away from the ball.

Transition Mismatch

Identifies size advantages in early offense before defense sets.

In-Game Application

Coaches can't always spot a mismatch when paying attention to the ball. By using Voop, mismatches will be labeled clearly at all corners of the court. The detector analyzes vertical separation and body positioning, triggering within seconds of mismatch formation. Coaches use this to instantly direct passes toward exploited matchups without pausing film. The system logs every forced mismatch, tracking how often your team hunts advantages versus settling for even matchups. After the game, reports show which players consistently created mismatches and which opponents were targeted most effectively.
