Pick & Roll Detection

Identifying screening actions and evaluating success in real time — with purple circles and path tracing.

Voop's pick-and-roll detector identifies screening actions when two offensive players stay within 100 pixels of each other for over half a second. The system draws purple circles around the screening action and traces the ball-handler's path. It then evaluates success by measuring shot quality improvement for the screener versus defender separation.

How It Works

📏 Proximity Detection

Two offensive players within 100 pixels triggers screening detection. YOLOv8 tracks player positions frame by frame.

⏱️ Duration Threshold

Players must stay within range for over half a second (15+ frames) to confirm intentional screen action.

🎨 Purple Circle Visualization

The system draws purple circles around the screening action in real time, making PnRs visible at a glance.

📈 Success Evaluation

Measures shot quality improvement for the screener and defender separation to determine effectiveness.

PnR Outcomes

SUCCESSFUL

Forced defensive rotation, created separation, improved shot quality.

NEUTRAL

Screen occurred but defense recovered; no significant advantage.

DEFENDED

Screen was ineffective; defender navigated or switched cleanly.

In-Game Application

Coaches see real-time indicators whether the PnR generated an advantage or was defended well. The detector tracks frequency and effectiveness, distinguishing between successful actions that forced defensive rotation versus stagnant screens. Post-game reports reveal which PnR combinations worked best, helping coaches design sets that exploit specific opponent coverages like drop coverage or switching defenses. This turns abstract scouting into actionable data.
