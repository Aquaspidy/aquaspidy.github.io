Voop App

Real-time computer vision basketball analytics for high school teams. Built with YOLOv8, PyQt, and optical flow tracking.

⚡

Possession Momentum

Tracks player movement to determine which team controls the tempo. Identifies scoring runs and momentum swings in real time →
🎯

Shot Quality (qSQ)

0-99 rating based on defender distance. Reveals whether your offense is generating good looks regardless of results →
🤝

Pick & Roll Detection

Identifies screening actions and evaluates success by measuring defender separation and shot quality improvement →
📏

Mismatch Hunter

Automatically highlights favorable defensive mismatches by comparing height and position →

Why these 4 features? Because we believe the essence of sports is and will always be human. No AI model can outpower a wise coach who knows their players. What AI can do is to help the coach to its best, detecting what the human eye can't always pay attention to and may be critical in helping a team win a game. This is why Voop focuses on these features above.
