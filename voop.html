Voop App

Real-time computer vision basketball analytics for high school teams. Built with YOLOv8, PyQt, and optical flow tracking.

Multi-Camera Support

Voop now supports up to 6 cameras simultaneously, providing comprehensive court coverage and enhanced accuracy. Multi-camera triangulation enables true 3D player tracking, precise shot quality analysis with real release height and angle, and eliminates blind spots that single-camera systems miss. Coaches can now view the entire court exhaustively — from baseline to baseline.

Heat Maps

Visualize player and team positioning intensity. See where your team operates best — and where opponents exploit you.

Learn more →

Why these features? Because we believe the essence of sports is and will always be human. No AI model can outpower a wise coach who knows their players. What AI can do is help the coach to its best — detecting what the human eye can't always pay attention to and what may be critical in helping a team win a game. This is why Voop focuses on these features above.
