What It Does

Voop's Events Detection module monitors the game in real time and identifies key moments that coaches and analysts need to track. The system detects Pick & Rolls, Fast Breaks, Drives, Passes, Steals, and Blocks — all in real time.

Each event is logged with a timestamp, the players involved, and contextual detail (e.g., "screen for #5 → defender switches"). Coaches can review the event timeline to understand the flow of the game, identify patterns, and make data-driven adjustments.

Unlike traditional stat tracking that requires manual entry, Voop's events are fully automatic — no clicking buttons or typing notes required. You just watch the game, and Voop captures everything.

How It Works

🔄 Pick & Roll

Detects two offensive players in close proximity (>0.5s). Evaluates success based on defender separation.

⚡ Fast Break

Detects possession change followed by rapid advance up the court (>3m/s).

🏃 Drive

Detects player moving fast (>3.5 km/h) toward basket. Tracks successful drives that create scoring opportunities.

✋ Steal & 🚫 Block

Detects possession change without pass (steal). Detects shot deflection near basket (block).

In-Game Application

During games, coaches can see every event as it happens — PnR frequency, fast break success rate, drive effectiveness, and defensive playmaking (steals and blocks). This real-time data helps coaches adjust on the fly.

For example, if a team is running PnR with high success, coaches can switch to a defensive scheme that counters it (e.g., switching all screens, dropping the big). If fast breaks are generating easy points, coaches can emphasize transition defense in the next timeout.

Post-game reports show event counts and success rates by quarter, lineup, and player — enabling detailed game analysis and practice planning. Coaches can see exactly what worked and what didn't.
