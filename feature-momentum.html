Possession Momentum

Tracking who controls the tempo — not just who has the ball.

The momentum detection feature in Voop tracks which team has control based on player movement. It analyzes horizontal motion of all players, calculating whether the offense is pushing forward or being driven back. When players move decisively toward the opponent's basket, momentum shifts toward that team.

How It Works

🎯 Decay Factor

Prevents sudden flips from single plays. A single highlight dunk won't swing momentum — sustained pressure is required.

📊 Threshold System

Ensures only sustained pressure changes possession. Temporary advantages are noted but don't flip control.

📈 Percentage Meter

Displays a 0-100% meter showing how strongly one team controls the tempo — a visual pulse of the game.

🔄 Optical Flow

Powered by computer vision that tracks horizontal motion vectors of every player on the court simultaneously.

In-Game Application

Coaches use momentum detection to identify scoring runs, recognize defensive stops before they show on the scoreboard, and determine the exact moment to call timeouts. Unlike basic possession arrows that just show who has the ball, Voop's momentum reveals the actual game flow and psychological advantage. This helps teams capitalize on hot streaks or strategically break opponent momentum with timely substitutions.
